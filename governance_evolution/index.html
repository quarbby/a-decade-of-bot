What a more nuanced governance would do

The two biggest governance-idea categories every year are blunt by design — Detection & Technical Countermeasures (48 papers, 2025) and General Countermeasures (54, 2025). What's growing faster, off a smaller base: Multi-Stakeholder Collaboration (0→7), Accountability & Liability (1→7), Ethical & Normative Design (1→9).

1. Disclosure, not a binaryTie detection to disclosed-vs-undisclosed, so conversational AI and influence bots stop being regulated identically.
2. Formalize the fastest-growing ideaMulti-stakeholder / co-governance categories are growing fastest from the smallest base — give them standing, not an afterthought.
3. Extend "international" downwardThe framing that already dominates platform governance should reach the under-studied subgroups — elder fraud, health-crisis misinformation.
