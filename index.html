Identification & screening funnel

Two identification streams, unioned by OpenAlex work ID: (1) four keyword-search query variants (title/abstract × singular/plural), and (2) a forward/backward citation snowball from 7 seed papers — "The rise of social bots" (2016), "Botometer 101: social bot practicum for computational social scientists" (2022), "BotOrNot" (2016), "A global comparison of social media bot and human characteristics" (2025), "Detection of Bots in Social Media: A Systematic Review" (2020), "Unpacking the Social Media Bot: A Typology to Guide Research and Policy" (2018), "The spread of low-credibility content by social bots" (2018) — via OpenAlex reference lists (backward) and the cites: filter (forward). Each record is then screened for whether it substantively concerns social media bots.

1,049 records had no retrievable abstract (excluded automatically); the remaining 4,502 were screened title+abstract against one criterion: is this substantively about social media bots, not a false-positive keyword match? The keyword-search stream (2,783 unique records) was screened by an LLM reading each abstract; the forward/backward snowball stream (2,768 records not already in the keyword-search corpus) was screened by a deterministic keyword-rule classifier over the same criterion, documented in forward_backward_review/scripts/annotate.py. Single database (OpenAlex) and title+abstract only — a first-pass reproducible scan, not a peer-reviewed systematic review.

Bot types, governance, and time

Among the 3,233 included papers, 948 mention at least one governance/policy idea. Bot personas are harmonized to Ng & Carley's Dual Personas of Social Media Bots taxonomy (arXiv:2504.12498), plus one added category — LLM Agent — for bots the paper explicitly describes as LLM/generative-AI-driven, a post-2023 wave the original 15-persona survey predates. Governance ideas are harmonized to a 14-category typology built from the raw phrasing (see the dataset's GOVERNANCE_TYPOLOGY.md). Categories beyond the top few (including the still-small LLM Agent count) are folded into “Other” per chart, but stay visible and filterable in the browse table below. 2026 is a partial year.
